Historical ball-by-ball data source: Cricsheet
https://cricsheet.org/downloads/

Downloaded 2026-10-19:
- Men's Test JSON: https://cricsheet.org/downloads/tests_male_json.zip
- Men's ODI JSON: https://cricsheet.org/downloads/odis_male_json.zip
- Men's T20I JSON: https://cricsheet.org/downloads/it20s_male_json.zip

Cricsheet data is licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License
(ODC-By 1.0): https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1.0/

The JSON source records were transformed into relational SQLite match,
batting, bowling, and fielding tables. Player roles/styles and venue country
or seating capacity are not supplied consistently in these source archives
and were not invented during import.
